The Electricity Ledger

Load your usage data

Every plan is priced against your own half-hourly meter readings, so you need a year of interval data. Two ways to get it: ask your retailer (most let you download it from your online account), or request it from your distributor — on the Ausgrid network that's the meter data request form, which emails you up to two years of data. Both the CSV retailers send and the official NEM12 file work here.
